What is Sufism?

Sufism (Tasawwuf) is the inner, mystical dimension of Islam. It focuses on purifying the heart, developing love for Allah, and following the spiritual path through practices like Dhikr, meditation, and self-discipline.

Divine Love

Developing unconditional love for Allah

Spiritual Purification

Cleansing the heart from negative traits

Universal Brotherhood

Love and service to all creation
